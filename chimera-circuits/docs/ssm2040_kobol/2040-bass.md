# 2040 EXP bass: A/B (ADR 0191, branch flt-2040-bass)

The 2040's EXP VOICE, before ("today", origin/main 2b7bd319, with STABILITY #715 and FB #719) and after ("new", flt-2040-bass 363af9ed, merged onto it). Rendered 2026-10-20 after the merge; FB off and no STABILITY spread, as the filter runs alone. Each file is 16-bit mono at 48 kHz. Every `_today` and `_new` file shares one gain, so the level differences you hear are real. REV2 is unchanged, bit for bit, so it has no files here.

What changed on EXP:
- A make-up gain that rises with RES: +5.0 dB at RES 0.4 and +6.9 dB at RES 1.
- The 3080 limit goes from ¼ to ½, so the ring is no longer squelched by a loud input.
- The cutoff trim is retuned.

## Listen in this order
1. `held_a1_res_sweep_{today,new}`: a 55 Hz saw at FREQ 1.5 kHz, RES 0 → 1 over 10 s, then held at 1. Today the bass sinks by about 14 dB as RES rises; new, it sinks by about 7. At the top, new keeps ringing over the note; today's ring is squelched.
2. `bassline_res60_*` and `bassline_res90_*`: a 16th-note A1 line with a decaying filter envelope, at RES 0.6 and 0.9.
3. Against the Kobol: `kobol_{sweep1,sweep_res5,sweep_res10}_{kobol,today,new}`.
   - `_kobol` is the raw take (MOTU input 5), with one gain across its three takes.
   - Ours follow the Kobol's inferred FREQ track (from `kobol-session/compare/sweeps.md`), on a plain saw at its 56.41 Hz, at RES 0, 0.4 and 1. Those are the matches by bump over the bass for knob 0, 5 and max.
   - Compare how much the bass drops from sweep1 to res5 to res10 within each source.

## Measured (full-scale A1 saw, FREQ 9.4 kHz, LEVEL ½; fundamental against RES 0)

| | RES 0.4 (knob 5) | RES 1 (max) |
|---|---|---|
| Kobol | −3.3 dB | −6.8 to −7.9 (≈ −7) |
| EXP today | −8.3 | −13.9 |
| EXP new | −3.3 | −7.0 |
| EXP new, 20 dB down | −3.5 | −7.1 |

- **Bump over the bass:** unchanged, within 0.3 dB at RES 0.4 and 0.85. The make-up lifts the whole response, as the Kobol's does.
- **Ring over a full-scale saw at RES 1** (FREQ 1 kHz): today −4 dB (squelched); new +5.7 dB. For reference, the Kobol reads +11 and REV2 +8.
- **Self-oscillation level:** today −17.1 dBFS; new −5.2 dBFS, in REV2's band.
- **Pitch:** within ±4.5 cents over C2–C6, after the trim.
- **Ring purity:** not addressed. The Kobol has H2 −39 dBc and H3 −44 dBc; EXP still has no H2 and H3 −60 dBc. Cell offsets are left for a follow-up.

## Caveat
The bass curve assumes that the Kobol's output level did not change between sweep1, res5 and res10 (`compare/notes.md`).
