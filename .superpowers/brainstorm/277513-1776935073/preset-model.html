Preset Model

What's your mental model for how presets work on Chimera?

A

Single Global Preset

One patch = one sound (engine + chain + effects + mod matrix). Like a Moog or Juno — save/load the entire synth state as one thing.

B

Multi-Part Preset

A "performance" contains 6 parts (one per chain/voice), each with its own engine + params. Like the original PreenFM3 — save/load the whole multi-timbral setup.

C

Both Levels

Individual part patches AND multi-part performances. Save a single sound, or save the whole 6-part setup. Like a Yamaha Motif or Nord.
