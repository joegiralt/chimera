# Per-Part MIDI Map (#247) Implementation Plan

**Goal:** Let a Part's own controller CCs move its Sound. The map lives in the Sound (16 entries), is learned with MIX+SEQ on the focused cell, and is listed on a MIDI MAP page. CC sweeps never snap or step.

**Architecture:**
- **Transport.** A pure `midi_in::route` splits MIDI at the ISR. Notes keep the note queue; CCs go to a per-source SPSC `CcRing`. The UI drains the rings each loop iteration.
- **Pure core.** The map types, the `Mappable` whitelist, the inter-arrival ramp and the stepped binning are pure code in `chimera-engine/src/midi_map/`.
- **UI shell.** `UiState` applies them through the encoder's own edit path, capped under `MOVES` a Part a pass. Learn is a small state machine beside the prime status.
- **Storage.** The map is stored as the non-critical Sound record `MidiMap` `0x001E`.

**Tech Stack:** Rust `no_std` (`chimera-engine`, `chimera-core`), `chimera-board`/`chimera-stm32` and `chimera-desktop` shells, the TLV card format (ADR 0045).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-13-midi-map-design.md` (owner ruling 2026-10-13). ADR: `docs/adr/0136-a-sound-carries-a-midi-map-learned-on-the-focused-cell.md`. Read the spec and `CLAUDE.md` before any task.

**Base and ordering.**
- The branch is `midi-map`, from `origin/main` d708d591.
- **If `all-notes-off` (CC 120/121/123) has merged, rebase before Task 1.** If not, build Task 1 as written. Whichever of the two lands second puts `ChannelMode` in `route`'s `Queue` arm (it is `from_midi`'s anyway).
- **`showcase-library` may merge first.** Then `0x001D` (`Note`) is on main: keep `MidiMap` at `0x001E`, and `once_bit`/`from_code` gain both arms. Task 10 is the coordination task for its factory Sources.

## Global Constraints

- **Code rules:**
  - No `unsafe` without `// SAFETY:`.
  - Nothing on the audio thread changes: no heap, no blocking and no new audio work.
  - The ISR does only `route` and a ring push.
  - No libc.
- **Never snap or step.** Continuous CC targets ramp over the inter-arrival time clamped to **15–120 ms** (15 ms after more than 120 ms of silence), and no publish carries more than `MOVES` knob changes a Part.
- **Type-driven:**
  - `Cc` (0..=127), `Mappable` (`ParamAddr` newtype, one fallible constructor) and `MapEntry` are newtypes.
  - The reserved CCs are one `const` table: 0, 6, 32, 38, 64, 96–101, 120–127.
  - Record codes are frozen: `MidiMap` = `0x001E`, not critical.
- **Neutral = empty map = no record.** `neutral_v1.txt` and every existing fixture stay byte-identical.
- **Labels and copy are exact as the spec:**
  - `MOVE A KNOB · <LABEL>`, `<LABEL> NOT MAPPABLE`, `CC <n> → <LABEL>`;
  - `CC <n> ON CH <c>, PART ON CH <p>` (1-based channels);
  - `CC <n> RESERVED`, `MAP FULL`;
  - the hint `MIX + SEQ` / `LEARNS A CC`;
  - the page row `MIDI MAP`, and its empty row `NO CCS · MIX+SEQ ON A CELL`.
- **Commits:** a terse subject, no type prefix. Stage by path. Never commit `chimera.bin`.
- **Build hygiene:**
  - After an engine edit, `touch chimera-engine/src/lib.rs` before tests (stale-build guard).
  - No worktree or ledger in `/tmp`.
- **Flash:** one line reporting the delta (`just firmware-orbit`, before Task 1 and after Task 9). The 5–8 KB in total is approved.
- **Green at the end:** `just check`, `just test` and `just clippy`.

## Review Focus

1. **A CC flood while a project loads.** The CC ring fills and drops, and the note queue never loses a note-on or note-off. Pinned by Task 1's `cc_flood_never_drops_note_ons`.
2. **Eight mapped knobs swept at once, plus a hand turn on a ninth in the same pass.** No publish exceeds `MOVES`, and no knob lands. Pinned by Task 5's `cc_sweep_never_snaps` (it turns encoder A each pass too).
3. **A CC arriving for a Part whose Sound was just replaced** (load, NEW, Revert). The old ramp doesn't drag the new Sound's value. Pinned by Task 5's `ramps_reset_on_load`.
4. **A map entry whose target the Part's engine doesn't read, after an engine switch.** It is kept, applied to nothing, and never touches another block. Pinned by Task 5's `dead_entries_after_engine_switch`.
5. **Learn armed, then the user leaves by B*n*, MENU or an ORBIT entry.** Learn cancels, and no later CC is swallowed as a learn. Pinned by Task 6's `learn_cancels_on_nav`.
