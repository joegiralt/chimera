# DRUM cost: block oscillators, a real bill, a load safety net

**Goal:** a burst of DRUM hits, alone or beside polyphonic Parts, never overruns the audio or resets the unit.

**Found on the unit (2026-11-14, ce1f5243, INIT DRUM, one hit alone):** pads 1–7 and 12 cost 1–12 % CPU. The open METAL HAT (pad 9) and TONE CYMBAL (pad 10) cost about 53 % each, and TONE COWBELL (pad 11) about 19 %. Eight overlapping hits overrun the audio, and twelve starve the controls loop until the IWDG resets the unit. Desktop (release, ns per block over idle): pads 1–7 and 12 cost 1,500–3,500, pads 8–10 cost 15,000–16,000 and pad 11 costs 6,400.

**Root cause:** `drum/kernel/mod.rs` `Pulse::tick` calls `VarOsc::tick_pulse`, which runs VA's whole `block()` + `run()` setup on a one-sample run, every sample. `metal.rs` `Bank` ticks 6 of these a sample (HAT, CYMBAL, and `Jingle`), and `Cowbell` ticks 2. Admission is by bill only, and DRUM's kernel is billed as ALGO (`engines.rs:604`, #951), so nothing refuses the hits.

## Global constraints
- Sound-identical: every golden stays bit-identical, or a change nulls below −120 dB against the old render. A change that can't do either goes to the owner's ear before it lands (memory: efficiency is sound-identical).
- No heap allocation and no blocking in audio. `// SAFETY:` on any unsafe.
- Bank 1 is near full (about 36 KB free on ORBIT). Report the ORBIT bank-1 delta. If it grows, add an ADR 0251 line.
- Commits: terse, no type prefixes. Never stage chimera.bin or docs/chimera-ui-ux-spec.md. No git stash.
- Builds: `` with `nice -n 15` and `CARGO_BUILD_JOBS=10`. Targeted tests in a task; `just check` before the task's commit.

## Task 1: the metal kernel's squares render a block at a time
- In `drum/kernel/mod.rs` add a block render on `Pulse`, `Pulse::block(&mut self, hz: f32, out: &mut Blk)`, built on VarOsc's block path. A `Bank`'s pitch is constant within a block (SHIMMER moves once a block), so `Bank` and `Jingle` call it once per square per block.
- `Cowbell`'s pitch moves every sample (BUMP). If VarOsc's ramped block path (inc start + step) nulls below −120 dB against today's render, use it. If not, keep the cowbell's sound and make its tick cheap some other sound-identical way (e.g. hoist the per-call setup out of `tick_pulse`). If neither works, stop and report with a render of both for the owner.
- Tests: a null test per metal machine (HAT closed and open, CYMBAL, COWBELL, JINGLE), new render vs old per-sample render, under −120 dB or bit-identical. Every golden unchanged (`golden_test`, including `drum_slice1_kit` and `drum_slice1_kit_lr`, which strike pads 8–10). The `chimera-utils drums::` nulls stay at −inf.
- Report desktop ns per block for every INIT DRUM pad, before and after (method: render INIT DRUM through `Instrument`, strike one pad, time the first 30 blocks over idle, release build).

## Task 2: bill DRUM by machine (#951)
- Replace the ALGO placeholder (`engines.rs:604`, `EngineType::Drum => AlgoEngine::cost(...)`) with a bill by machine. A hit's bill is its pad's machine cost; the Part's admission bill is the costliest machine in its kit (the kit's pads are known; `KitBill` already carries the pads' filter shapes).
- The costs come from the unit, measured by the controller after Task 1 is flashed, and are given to the implementer as `Cost` values with their method. Each constant's comment names the measurement, the date and the build, as `instrument.rs` `BURST` does.
- Tests: the bill of every machine is at least its measured cost; INIT DRUM's 12 pads struck in 60 ms steps on the desktop never bill past the budget (`SampleBudget::for_cpu(CPU_HZ_REV_V)`).

## Task 3: the load safety net
- An ADR (next number): voices are admitted by bill (unchanged), and as a backstop, when a rendered block's measured cycles pass 95 % of the block's budget, the Instrument sheds one voice before the next block (the one `shed` already picks, fading as a shed does). One shed per overloaded block, so a burst sheds until the load is back under.
- `chimera-engine`: `Instrument::shed_for_load(&mut self)`, host-testable. `chimera-stm32`: after `probe::measure` of a half, compare the cycles with the block budget and call it. Threshold a named constant.
- Tests (host): with a fake load signal over the threshold, one voice is shed per block until none is left to shed; under it, nothing changes, and the goldens stay bit-identical.
- Unit gate (controller): a 12-pad roll at 60 ms, and DRUM on Part 1 with chords on Parts 2–3, give no reset and no overrun.
