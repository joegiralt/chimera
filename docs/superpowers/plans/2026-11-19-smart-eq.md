# SMART EQ plan (#396, ADR 0296)

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-19-smart-eq-design.md`. Worktree
(private notes), branch `smart-eq` from origin/0.4 b136ef37.
Builds: `CARGO_BUILD_JOBS=6 CARGO_INCREMENTAL=0 nice -n 15 <cmd>`.
No stash, no `cargo fmt --all`, never stage chimera.bin.

1. **Types** (`part_eq/smart.rs`, bank 1 data and inline helpers): `Role`
   (FRONT, MID, BACK; codes 0, 1, 2; `MID` default; `outranks`), `Band`
   (LOW, LOW MID, HIGH MID, HIGH; `ALL`, `centre`), `CutDb` (0..=6 dB),
   `Amount` (0..=127, `Unit`), `Switch` (OFF, ON), `SmartEq` (one byte:
   ON bit 7, AMOUNT bits 0–6; `OFF` default, AMOUNT 64), `SmartState`
   (all-zero rest), `CutView` (the UI's packed live cuts). Tests:
   `smart_eq_test.rs` (codes, byte totality, rest is zero bytes).
2. **Settings**: `PartParams::role` (id 17, ROLE choice), `CompParams::smart`
   (ids 8 SMART EQ choice, 9 AMOUNT stepped); get/write/enum arms; sizes
   unchanged (20 B, 32 B); disk-code and NRPN fixtures; `mappable` AMOUNT
   only; test_support's full project sets them. Test: save/load and an
   old project's load (`project_codec_test`).
3. **DSP** (`part_eq/dsp/smart.rs`, test-first in `smart_eq_dsp_test.rs`):
   the four SVF bands on bus and side, levels, the rule, the slews, the
   lerped gains, unity bands never write, the rest. Tests: clash cuts the
   pad's LOW not the bass; FRONT untouched bit for bit; step bound; no
   subnormals; OFF releases then rests.
4. **Render**: `dsp::run_parts` runs SMART EQ after LO CUT and TILT;
   `PartsBlock` carries `smart`; bank 1's stub skips while all quiet.
   Tests (`smart_eq_render_test.rs`): OFF and AMOUNT 0 bit for bit on
   multi-Part material; ON changes only the lower roles.
5. **UI**: ROLE cell C of PRT › EQ; MST › SMART EQ page (id 120) with SMART
   EQ and AMOUNT; `VizType::SmartEq` drawn by `UiDoor::smart_eq` (live
   bars on PRT › EQ, the coarse grid on MST › SMART EQ); live cuts
   published by the audio (`part_eq::SMART_VIEW`). Suite fixtures (nav,
   d-pad, glyph, declarations, screen goldens) follow.
6. **Bench and budgets**: ROUTING rows `SMART` (six Parts, ON, AMOUNT 127);
   host timing; `just check`, `just firmware-orbit`; bank 1 and bank 2
   lines raised by measured deltas only; ITCM resident size; ADR 0296's
   Measurements.
7. **Tuning** on multi-Part material (drum demos, the teaser vignettes):
   cut trajectories and band levels, ON vs OFF.
