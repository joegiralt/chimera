# 0254. Give MIXER, SEQUENCER and AUTO their own channels, off by default

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Deciders:** owner (#808: the set and AUTO), coordinator (defaults, precedence, scope)

## Context
#808 asks for Elektron's split: track channels, an FX/mixer control channel and an auto channel. The FX have no Part channel to be mapped on, and ORBIT had no MIDI control but SYNC's transport. The issue proposed MIXER 14, SEQUENCER 15, AUTO 16 as defaults, but MPE (#896, ADR 0252) uses 16 as its upper zone's master and 2-15 as its lower zone's members, and a channel taken by default would steal notes from a setup that used it before.

## Decision
- **Settings.** `MidiSettings::control: u16` (MIXER, SEQUENCER, AUTO, five bits each from bit 0; 0 OFF, else channel + 1), params 9, 10, 11 of the `Midi` block (`MIXER_CH`, `SEQ_CH`, `AUTO_CH`; 4 is PROGRAM IN's, #891, 5-8 MPE's, #896). All three default OFF, and a SYSTEM from before them loads OFF. Set on SETTINGS › MIDI CONFIG › CONTROL CHANNELS (`leaves::CONTROL`, page id 114, never used, past MPE's 113, so `MAX_PAGES` grows by one byte), between CHANNELS and ECHO.
- **Precedence.** A Part's own channel beats everything, then an MPE member channel (to be wired when #896 lands: `control::control_on` yields to it, as `cc_mpe` already runs before `cc_dedicated`), then MIXER, SEQUENCER, AUTO in that order. A CONTROL channel that loses reads dimmed on its page (`SlotCtx::clash`, who wins two bits a channel), and the focus band names the winner: `A PART HAS THIS CHANNEL`, `MIXER HAS THIS CHANNEL` or `SEQUENCER HAS THIS CHANNEL`.
- **MIXER** (fixed chart, `control::mixer_target`, published in `docs/guide/MIDI.md` and pinned by `midi_control_test`): CC 20-25 Part 1-6 LEVEL, 26-31 PAN, 41-46 CHORUS send, 47-52 DELAY send, 53-58 REVERB send, 70-94 the CHORUS, DELAY and REVERB knobs and 102-108 COMP's, 32 in a fixed table (a new FX param takes a CC past them, never one of these). Every chart CC is a `FreeCc`: never 0, 6, 32, 38, 64 or 96-101, which MIDI in keeps for bank select, data entry, its increment and decrement, the RPN/NRPN selects and the pedal (a test asserts it, and one sends a CC through `DinPipe` end to end). An FX block's NRPN moves it too. Each moves through the Part's canonical slot (`cc_canonical`), so it ramps, bins and counts against the Part's moves in flight exactly as an unbound NRPN on a Part channel; the FX through Part 1's slots. Notes on MIXER are ignored. There is no Part MUTE yet: #898.
- **SEQUENCER** (`control::seq_target`): CC 20 PLAY/STOP, 21-25 ring 1-5 ON/OFF (MUTE), each on a rise past 63 (a momentary button is one press, `MidiUi::seq_high`); 26-30 ring CHANCE, 102-106 ring STEPS, 107 KEY, 108 SCALE, each binned over its steps with ADR 0139's hysteresis and written through `OrbitSong::edit`. A note-on on SEQUENCER's channel sets KEY to its pitch class: the pipe raises `CtrlEvent::Key` (ring tag 8) instead of queueing the note, so it plays nothing.
- **AUTO.** The pipe (`DinPipe`, both DIN and USB) moves every channel voice and channel mode message on AUTO's channel to the focused Part's channel (`ROUTES`, a relaxed `AtomicU32` the UI stores each pass, as `PART_CHANNELS`, read in one load), so AUTO plays every Part on the focused Part's channel and its map answers the CCs. Held notes follow Elektron's auto channel: a note-on or pedal-down plays the newly focused Part at once, even with the pedal held; only a note-off (and a held key's poly pressure) reaches the Part its note started on, and a pedal-up reaches every Part a pedal-down went to. `AutoLatch` keeps each key's source and Part channel, the pedalled channels and the pedal's source, and is checked before `ROUTES`, so turning AUTO OFF or moving it to another channel mid-note never hangs a note, and a raw note-off of the same key on another channel stays that channel's. A channel mode on AUTO reaches the focus and every Part AUTO holds keys on from that channel; All Notes Off, All Sound Off, Omni Off/On, Mono On and Poly On clear those keys, Reset All Controllers the pedals; System Reset and `lost` clear it all.
- **Fixed maps only.** Learn never binds on MIXER's or SEQUENCER's channel. Picking a controller map (#807) there is #897. USB named cables per CONTROL channel are later.
- **Flash.** All behaviour is bank 2's: `ui::midi::control` (the MIDI UI's glob), the pipe's routing (`DinPipe`'s), and the clash words and check through two new `MidiUiDoor` entries (`clashes`, `clash_note`, the latter taking the cell's address and `SlotCtx::clash`; the door's magic is now `0xB2C0_0254`). Bank 1 keeps what SYSTEM's codec needs with the door closed and the stubs, measured against main 4280503f with `CHIMERA_STAMP_CLEAN=1`: +776 B default, +864 B ORBIT, +784 B bank2-gate, +768 B usb-audio, +400 to +680 B on the bench builds, each line of `tools/bank1-budget.txt` raised by exactly its measure, naming this ADR. Roughly: ~580 B data (the three specs ~216 B, their OFF/1-16 names ~136 B, the spec switch ~32 B, the page ~120 B, its leaf, blocks and the MIDI CONFIG row ~76 B); the Midi block's get/write ~116 B (get ~28, write ~88, the packed `u16`'s shifts); the clash stubs ~110 B (`view::clash_note` ~56, `UiState::ctx`'s door call ~52); the main loop (`chimera_board::synth`) ~196 B of inliner churn around the 24-byte `SystemSettings` copied at boot; less ~340 B saved by SYSTEM's `sync` writing the settings in place rather than moving a `SystemSettings` out through its closure. Bank 2 grows +2,976 B on ORBIT's build (the same default), leaving ORBIT's bank 2 12,288 B free (ADR 0251's line is 8 KB). RAM: +320 B `.bss` in the shipped builds (each pipe's `AutoLatch` with its 128-key table and held bits, `ROUTES`; a bench build, deaf, has no key table: +56 B), +8 B D2 (`MidiUi::seq_high`).

## Alternatives considered
- **Defaults 14, 15, 16** (the issue's): collide with MPE's zones and silently take channels a setup may use. OFF costs one visit to SETTINGS.
- **Fold the three into the CHANNELS page:** its six cells are the six Parts'.
- **AUTO as a Part filter in the audio** (only the focused Part plays, not every Part on its channel): needs the instrument's note path and AudioShared to learn the focus. Rewriting the channel in the pipe is a few bytes of bank 2 and behaves as the hardware precedent.
- **MIXER through the Part maps' machinery with a built-in map:** the canonical NRPN path already ramps any NRPN-addressed param; a CC-to-NRPN table is all MIXER needs.
- **Absolute CC for PLAY/STOP and ring ON/OFF** (≥ 64 on): a momentary button would start and stop at once. A rising edge acts as the panel keys do.

## Consequences
- `MidiSettings` grows from 3 to 6 bytes (a `u16` of channels); SYSTEM stores the three by ident, and an old SYSTEM loads them OFF.
- A Part moved onto a CONTROL channel silently takes it; the page marks it.
- MPE's PR must make `control_on` yield to member channels when it lands.
