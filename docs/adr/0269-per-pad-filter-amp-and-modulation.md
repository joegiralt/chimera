# 0269. Give each DRUM pad its own FILTER, AMP, mod envelope, LFO and four routes

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Deciders:** owner (2026-11-10 ruling 4, compact pads; 2026-11-13 slice order and AMP MODE; 2026-11-14 VEL is the pad's kernel level), firmware

## Context
Slice 1 (ADR 0265) gave a DRUM Part a kit of 12 pads, each with a machine, eight knobs, level, pan, choke and RETRIG, but one FILTER and one AMP for the whole Part: every pad of a kit went through the same cutoff and the same VCA. A drum machine's pads each shape their own sound (the Rytm's FILTER and AMP pages are per pad), and the drum spec's "The voice" and owner ruling 4 ask for a compact Rytm-style pad: its own filter and amp, one mod envelope, one LFO, a few routes. The pad must stay small, as a kit lives in ITCM (`Kits`, `KIT_VIEW`; ADR 0266) where ~51 KB is free, while D2 is full and AXI sits at its margin; and a slice-1 kit must keep sounding bit for bit as it does.

## Decision
- **A pad carries a `PadSound`** (`dsp::drum::sound`, every field private and typed): `PadFilter` (KIND, MODE, VOICE, CUTOFF `Hz`, RESONANCE and MORPH `Unit`: the FLT front's cells, `PadFilter::IDS`; MODE always one of KIND's, by `FilterParams::set_kind`'s rule), `PadAmp` (an AHD `PadEnv`, VEL, the gate's LPG, DECAY and STRIKE as `lpg::Knobs`, and MODE), a mod `PadEnv`, a `PadLfo` (WAVE `LfoShape`, SPEED, DEPTH, PHASE, MODE `LfoMode { Free, Trig }`) and `PadRoutes`, four `PadRoute`s. `Pad::new` gives `PadSound::DEFAULT`; `Pad::with_sound`, `sound`, `sound_mut`.
- **Overlaid on the voice's `played` snapshot.** On a DRUM voice the pad's FILTER and AMP cells are laid over `played` right after the Part's knobs (`PadSound::overlay`: `PadFilter::apply`, `out.gate`). From there the Sound chain runs unchanged. `PadSound::from_sound` takes a Sound's FILTER and AMP cells (the rest `DEFAULT`'s), and its overlay is the identity bit for bit, so a pad copied from its Part's Sound nulls against slice 1.
- **Shared by the Part, not the pad:** the MTX, E1–E3 and L1–L3, the FX slot, SENDS, and the filter's INPUT, FB, STABILITY and jacks.
- **The AMP envelope is AHD:** attack, hold, decay (127 is `EnvDecay::Infinite`: the level holds), SPEED FAST/MED/SLOW as Envelope A's; it runs as Envelope A with sustain 0 and release the decay (`PadEnv::env_params`). `PadEnv::OPEN` (A 0, H 0, D ∞) is slice 1's AMP: a constant 1.
- **AMP MODE** (`AmpMode`, codes 1 ONE-SHOT, 2 GATED, fixed by hand; an unknown code loads the default's): ONE-SHOT, the default, ignores note-off, so a pad's AMP plays out as in slice 1 and note-off is heard only by the Part's VCA routes; GATED plays the attack and hold, holds full until note-off (a short note still plays its attack and hold first), then releases over DECAY; an infinite DECAY never releases. The precedent is the Elektron AMP envelope's mode (the Rytm's and the Digitakt's AMP page).
- **Four routes**, `RouteId` 0..=3, each `source → dest` by `Amount` −127..=127. Sources: MOD ENV, LFO, VEL (the pad's) and LFO 1–3 (the Part's shared LFOs). Destinations: CUTOFF, RESO, VOLUME, LFO SPEED, LFO DEPTH. Codes fixed by hand, 1-based, 0 never used. `PadRoute::OFF` is MOD ENV → CUTOFF at 0. A route moves the filter at audio rate, as a Part route does.
- **VEL is the pad's kernel level** (owner 2026-11-14, controller ruling the same day). A hit's kernel plays at g = 1 − VEL·(1 − v), v its velocity as 0..1, computed as v + (1 − VEL)·(1 − v) so both ends are exact: VEL 1 is slice 1's own law (the kernel's v, bit for bit), VEL 0 ignores velocity. It replaces the kernel's raw velocity scale (`KernelState::strike_in_place` takes the level), so it acts before the filter: a soft hit drives LADDER or DIRTY less, as on an analog voice. g is latched at the strike, like the machine's knobs: a VEL turn reaches the next hit. Gliding it on a ringing hit would mean reaching into every kernel's state with a per-sample ramp in its maths, which the nulls forbid. The overlay leaves `out.vca_vel`, so a Part VCA route keeps the Sound's own VEL, and the pad's VEL cell is never dimmed. DRUM INIT's pads take its Sound's VEL, 1; a slice-1 kit's pads take their Sound's through `from_sound`.
- **VOLUME** is a gain on the hit after its AMP envelope, never over unity. Each VOLUME route sets its own depth from unity: with a = amount / 127 and its source's value v read as 0..1 (a bipolar LFO as (v + 1) / 2), a > 0 opens with the source, g = 1 − a·(1 − v) (the Part VCA's polarity); a < 0 ducks, g = 1 − |a|·v; a = 0 is 1. The pad's VOLUME gain is the product of its routes' gains, held to 0..1, and ramps per sample from the last block's. Every amount is heard; nothing boosts.
- **Latched or live, the Rytm's hybrid.** Choices latch at the hit: filter TYPE and MODE, LFO WAVE and MODE, a route's SOURCE and DEST, env SPEED, AMP MODE. Continuous values follow live edits of the pad on its sounding hits, glided (never snapped): CUTOFF, RES, env times, LPG, LFO SPEED and DEPTH, route AMOUNT. The Rytm's FILTER and AMP knobs move a hit that is still ringing.
- **How a hit follows.** The voice re-reads its pad's sound from the view after a publish: `KitView::sound_if_newer` is one `seq` load a block against the `seq` the hit saw at its strike, and decodes only after a publish, never waiting. `PadSound::live_target` keeps the hit's choices and takes the pad's continuous values; `DrumEngine::follow`, once a block before the overlay, glides them there on their 0..1 places (`Places`: CUTOFF, RES and MORPH on their `FILTER_SPECS` laws, CUTOFF's octaves included; VEL, LPG, DECAY, STRIKE; both envelopes' A, H, D; LFO SPEED, DEPTH, PHASE; each route's AMOUNT) at the KNOB coefficient, as a Part's knob glides (ADR 0112, 0116), and lands on the pad's values bit for bit. The followed CUTOFF reaches the filter as a Part's knob CUTOFF does: a block's value, ramped in by the filter's knob-glide path (ADR 0117), or per sample while a route moves the filter. The AMP and mod envelopes' stages (`ACoefs`) are rebuilt only in a block their places moved. A DECAY's hold (127) is a choice: a held decay stays held, a timed one stays timed. A hit struck with an OPEN AMP, or with no route, keeps none until its next hit. The machine's knobs, VEL, LEVEL and PAN stay latched (VEL's place still glides, but nothing reads it after the strike). A kit not edited costs a load a hit a block.
- **The gate is the pad's.** A pad's LPG, DECAY and STRIKE replace the Part's gate on its hits (`overlay` writes `out.gate`); the Sound's own are masked on DRUM, as its FILTER KIND is.
- **Billed by the pads.** A DRUM Part's voice is billed `Voice::drum_cost` with its kit's `KitBill`, published beside the pads (`KitView::bill`): the filter at the costliest shape (KIND, and VOICE on the 2040) its pads play, with STAB, FB, LEVEL and the moving path as that shape bills them and a pad route's move once (the Sound's own shape only before the kit's first publish); `LPG_COST` while any pad's gate is above 0 or a Part route moves LPG, DECAY or STRIKE; and the costliest pad's AMP and routes (20 an AMP alone, 55 routed: placeholders until the unit bench, #951). A slice-1 kit bills exactly as its Sound did.
- **The pad MOD page** is ordered PENV, PLFO, ROUTE. The FX slot stays the Part's: there is no per-pad FX.
- **Bytes.** `PadSound::to_bytes` is 57 B (`PAD_SOUND_BYTES`): filter 15 (KIND, MODE, VOICE by their disk codes, then CUTOFF, RES, MORPH as f32 LE), AMP 21 (A, H, D, SPEED, MODE, then VEL, LPG, DECAY, STRIKE as f32 LE), mod env 4, LFO 5 (WAVE, SPEED, DEPTH, PHASE, MODE), routes 12 (SOURCE, DEST, AMOUNT ×4). `from_bytes` is total: an unknown code or a non-finite float takes the default's field, a value past its range is held to it, a MODE the KIND lacks is the default's if the KIND has it, else the KIND's default. `Pad::to_bytes`/`from_bytes` stay 13 B (the `Kit` record's pad is frozen) and keep the default's sound; a pad's sound is saved in its own `KitSound` record (next).
- **Saved as a `KitSound` record a pad** (0x0025, not critical), after the Part's `Kit`: the pad's index 0–11, then its 57 B, 58 B a record. Every pad's record is always written, even at `DEFAULT`: sparse writing is ruled out, as on a DRUM Part an absent record means the slice-1 rule below. A wrong length or an index of 12 or more is `Bounds`, a pad's second record `Corrupt`; an unknown code or a non-finite float takes `DEFAULT`'s field and makes the load lossy. A pad with no record keeps `DEFAULT`, except on a Part with a `Kit` whose Sound is DRUM (a slice-1 file): there it takes `PadSound::from_sound` of the Part's Sound, settled at the Part's end once that Sound is finished (its refused codes retried), so the kit sounds as it did. Neither absent case makes the load lossy. The new codes are frozen in `disk_codes_v1.txt` (`C DRUM_ROUTE_SOURCE`, `DRUM_ROUTE_DEST`, `DRUM_LFO_MODE`, `DRUM_AMP_MODE`).
- **`PadSound::DEFAULT`** is DRUM INIT's Sound (CLEAN LP4 at 20 kHz, RES 0, VEL 1, no gate) with an open AMP on ONE-SHOT, a mod envelope A 0 H 0 D 64 MED, the default LFO (SINE, SPEED 6, DEPTH 127, PHASE 0, FREE) and no routes.
- **Memory, in ITCM only.** `PadSound` is 64 B (57 of values, padded to its f32s' alignment), a `Pad` 80 B (13 before), a `Kit` 964 B. `Kits`: 990 B → 5,828 B (~1.0 → ~5.7 KB). `KIT_VIEW`, once it publishes the 57 B sound beside each pad's 13 in both seqlock slots: 1,968 B → 10,200 B (1.9 → ~10.0 KB). ITCM grows ~13.0 KB of its ~51 KB free; D2 and AXI do not grow.
- **Flash.** DRUM is bank 1's (ADR 0251, 0265); slice 2 is estimated at +9–14 KB of bank 1, each task's delta measured and its line raised under ADR 0251.

## Alternatives considered
- **A full per-pad Sound (the Part's whole chain a pad):** ~1 KB a pad, ~72 KB the six kits; no room in ITCM, and the compact pad is the owner's ruling 4.
- **A per-pad FX slot:** the FX slot is a voice's costliest block and the Part's character; a pad's FX would multiply the bill by 12. It stays Part-wide.
- **Everything latched at the hit:** a ringing hit would not answer a filter sweep, unlike the Rytm; kept only as the fallback if the glide task is cut.
- **Everything live:** a TYPE, MODE or route SOURCE change on a ringing hit would be a click or a jump; choices latch.
- **AMP always one-shot (no MODE):** a held pad (a pad played as a drone, a gated hat) could not be shortened by its key; the Elektron AMP mode is the known answer.
- **One `KitSound` record a Part (684 B):** past `MAX_RECORD_LEN` (640 B), which sizes every record buffer on the stack; raising it grows each of them.
- **Pad sound bytes in the `Kit` record's pad (13 B → 70 B):** the record's pad is frozen (ADR 0045); the sound is saved by its own rule.

## Consequences
- Each pad shapes its own sound; every non-DRUM Sound sounds exactly as before (goldens, drum nulls). A slice-1 kit did too until the VEL ruling, its one deliberate change: its hits' level follows the pad's VEL law (the fixture's VEL 0.6, `drum_slice1_kit` re-recorded); at VEL 1 it renders slice 1 bit for bit (`drum_slice1_vel_test`).
- `Pad`, `Kit` and `DrumHit` grow: a hit copies ~80 B, so `DrumHit` is passed by reference on the stack-checked path.
- ITCM's free room falls to 48,480 B (measured below; the ~51 KB free before was an underestimate).
- **Measured at the slice's end** (ORBIT release build, `just check` green): bank 1 877,532 B, +19,720 B over slice 1's 857,812 (over the +9–14 KB estimate: the pad pages, cells and live follow cost more than the types), 39,972 B under the 917,504 B SD-flash limit; bank 2 515,712 B of 524,288, 8,576 B free; `.itcm` 16,032 B of 64,512 (`Kits` 5,828 + `KIT_VIEW` 10,200), 48,480 B free; stack, nested, ORBIT 62,840 B and default 62,592 B of the 63,232 B line.
- The pad's routes and LFO are a second, smaller modulation layer beside the Part's: the Part's MTX still reaches the overlaid filter and VCA.
