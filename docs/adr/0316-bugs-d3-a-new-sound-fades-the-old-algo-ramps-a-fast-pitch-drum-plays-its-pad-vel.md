# 0316. The bugs-d3 batch: a new Sound fades the old, ALGO ramps a fast pitch, DRUM plays its pad's VEL

- **Status:** Accepted (2026-11-22)
- **Deciders:** owner (2026-11-22, "go with your recommendations" on #14, #164, #963); firmware (the 0.4 bug sweep's batch D3); the owner hears the before/after renders

## Context
Three sound defects left on origin/0.4 after batch D (ADR 0310):

- #14: a Part's new Sound under a held chord faded each voice out and restarted its held note on the new Sound (an engine or model change), or let it play on under the new Sound's settings (the same engine).
- #164: ALGO steps its operators' increments once a block (ADR 0042's choice): an ENV or LFO on PITCH moves several semitones a block, and the steps are heard.
- #963: on DRUM the Sound's AMP VEL (no DRUM page shows it, DRUM INIT stores 1) still scaled a hit under a Part VCA route, on top of the pad's VEL; a pad's STRIKE read the raw velocity, so VEL 0 ignored velocity in level only.

## Decision
- **A new Sound fades the old (#14).** When a Part's `loads` move (a program change, a library load, INIT; `Project::load_part` moves them only once the load lands, so a refused one fades nothing), `Instrument::fade_replaced`, a bank 1 stub through `AdmitDoor::fade_replaced` to `part_sound::fade_replaced_in` (bank 2's, beside batch E's `sym_admit`), takes each of the Part's held voices (a key or the pedal), sounding and not already fading (or fading toward a restart), through `sound_off`: `Voice::FADE`, the slot dying, as CC 120 does. Nothing restarts and nothing plays on. A released tail rings on, landed on the new Sound's knobs, as before. It runs before a block's notes (`handle_streamed`) and before its render, so a note in the block the Sound lands in plays the new Sound. An engine or model change made by editing the Sound (its `loads` unmoved) restarts held notes as before (#33's M6).
- **ALGO ramps a fast pitch (#164).** When the ENVs' and LFOs' (`ModSource::is_fast`) routes into PITCH and FINE can move a note more than a semitone (`engines::pitch_ramps`, over `pitch_reach_by` with only the fast sources counted; taken once a publish in `Instrument::recost` through `AdmitDoor::ramps`, which returns the Parts' mask and their ramp bills together (`instrument::part_sound`, bank 2's), and handed to each voice, `Voice::set_pitch_ramps`). The predicate reads the routes' reach only, never a source's depth: WHEEL, PRESS and SLIDE on PITCH never ramp and step per block as before, each sounding table operator starts the block at the increment of the last block's pitch (`AlgoEngine::ramp_from`, `None` from a note-on) and steps `(to − from)/64` a sample (a signed `i32`) (`Kernel::render_ramped`; every loop adds its lane's step, 0 when steady, so one loop serves both and the pairs stay paired). Formant and noise operators keep their block's increment. Without a fast route, or on a note's first block, the block is the old one, bit for bit. Billed: `AlgoEngine::COST_RAMP`, 2 cycles a sample for every table operator (`ramp_cost`: the start increment taken each block; a silent operator takes none, so the bill is an upper bound); the step's integer add, run on every table operator, is left in `COST_OP` (one add in a ~40-instruction loop, inside its measured margin; a bench row would confirm it), on any Sound whose fast pitch routes pass the semitone; pinned in `algo_pitch_ramp_test` and `cost_test`. INIT's and the factory Sounds' wheel vibrato (LFO 1 → PITCH at 2, 38 cents) steps as before, unbilled: ADR 0042's vibrato.
- **DRUM plays its pad's VEL (#963).** `PadSound::overlay` sets the played Sound's AMP VEL to 0, so the voice's VCA routes and the gate take no velocity of their own: the pad's VEL at the kernel (`PadAmp::level`) is the hit's one velocity law. A DRUM hit's STRIKE strikes at full height (`lpg::Drive::hit`, now a `Unit`, is 1 for DRUM): the velocity is already in the kernel's level, so the law applies once, and at VEL 0 every velocity strikes alike.

## Alternatives considered
- #14: let held notes finish on the old Sound (two Sounds a Part at once); fade one block and restart (the old behaviour, a retrigger).
- #164: ramped copies of the operator loops (6.6 KB of bank 2: ORBIT's S0–S3 overflowed by 160 B); ramp on any pitch change (unbilled under the wheel); ramp formant operators too (their grain clocks latch per block).
- #963: showing the Sound's AMP VEL on DRUM (two VELs for one hit).

## Consequences
Sound changes only on the fixed paths: a Part's held voices at a new Sound; ALGO under an ENV or LFO on PITCH or FINE; DRUM hits with a VCA route or STRIKE. Not here: #695 (CLEAN LP4 fully open), its own PR (ADR 0327); and #352 (the sine table's fold), merged first in its own (ADR 0325), these goldens re-taken on top of it. Flash over origin/0.4 a4e15f2b0: bank 1 +336 B (default; +208 to +544 over the other sets): the two AdmitDoor stubs, the ramp mask and bills folded into `recost`, and ALGO's per-voice ramp flag; `sound_off` stays inline in bank 1, as 0.4 had it. Bank 2 +1856 B (default; ORBIT +2176, S0-S3 +896 B of it, 448 B left there (0.4 had 1344)): `part_sound`'s fade and ramps beside `sym_admit` in S4, and ALGO's ramped pitch in its block. Two AdmitDoor entries, no new door. RAM: `faded` and `ramps` in the Instrument, a byte a Part and one more; ORBIT links (D2). Re-recorded goldens: `golden_test`'s `factory_11` (#164, its ENV 3 → PITCH swoop) and `drum_slice1_kit`, `drum_slice1_kit_lr` (#963, STRIKE on VEL 0.6 pads); `algo_door_test`'s case 12 (#164). With the three behaviours switched off, every golden of origin/0.4 passes unchanged. The renders are in (private notes).
